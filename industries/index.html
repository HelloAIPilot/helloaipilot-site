DENTAL PRACTICES

Dental AI Receptionist

While your team is chairside, the phone keeps ringing. Your receptionist books new and returning patients, answers insurance and hours questions, and sends confirmations, so open slots get filled.

What it handles

  • New-patient, cleaning and follow-up appointments
  • Insurance accepted, hours and directions

Hotel AI Receptionist

Guests call to ask about rooms, rates, parking and check-in times, often late at night. Your receptionist answers on the first ring, captures reservation requests, and passes special situations to your staff.

What it handles

  • Room availability, rates and room types
  • Reservation requests with dates and guest count

Rules it follows

Real Estate AI Receptionist
