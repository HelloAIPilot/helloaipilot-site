WHY WE BUILT IT

A good receptionist shouldn't depend on who's at the desk.

Most small businesses lose calls for the same few reasons: the phone rings during a job, the front desk is busy, or nobody is in after five. HelloAIPilot covers those gaps without changing how your team works.

What we commit to

  • Nothing goes live until you have approved the script and made test calls
  • Your receptionist answers only from information you approve
  • You get a summary of every call, so nothing is lost in a voicemail
  • We keep tuning after launch as your calls change
HOW WE WORK

Set up carefully. Tuned after launch.

Personal setup

The team writes your receptionist's answers and rules with you. Nothing goes live until you have approved the script and made test calls.

Only approved answers

Your receptionist speaks from the information you give it. When a question is outside that, it passes it to a person instead of guessing.

Built around your calls

After launch we review real calls with you and adjust answers, rules and hand-offs until the receptionist matches how your business works.
