WHY IT MATTERS

Every unanswered call is a customer
calling your competitor next.

After hours

Nights and weekends go to voicemail

AC breakdowns, roof leaks and toothaches don't wait for business hours. Most callers who reach a voicemail don't leave a message. They call the next business on the list.

Busy hours

Your team can't answer and serve at once

When the front desk is checking in a guest or a patient, the phone keeps ringing. Every missed ring during your busiest hour is a booking you paid to attract.

Slow follow-up

The first business to answer usually wins

Buyers, homeowners and guests rarely wait for a callback. A real estate lead or a storm-damage call goes to whoever picks up first.

HOW A CALL GOES

From first ring to booked in one call.

Greets and listens

The caller hears your business name, then describes what they need. Questions can come in any order, and the receptionist keeps up.

Checks your rules

It answers from the information you approved and recognises calls that need a person, such as emergencies, complaints and billing.
