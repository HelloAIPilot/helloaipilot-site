FAQ

Common questions from business owners.

Still have a question? Email chandra@helloaipilot.com or call +1 346-889-6827.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your current number and forward calls to your AI receptionist, either all the time, after hours, or only when your team doesn't pick up.

What happens when a caller needs a real person?

You decide. Emergencies, complaints, billing questions and anything else you choose can be transferred to your team or turned into an urgent callback with a text alert.

Will it give wrong prices or make promises?

It answers only from the information you approve. If a question is outside that, it says a team member will follow up rather than guess.

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Yes. We connect it to your scheduling tool so it sees live availability, books the slot, and sends the caller a confirmation by email or text.

Will this replace my front-desk staff?

It's built to back them up. It catches the calls they can't get to, after hours and during busy times, so your team can focus on the customers in front of them.

Will callers know they are speaking to an AI?

Yes. The receptionist introduces itself as your business's AI assistant, so callers always know what they are talking to.

Can my team see what was said on each call?

Yes. Every call gets a summary with the caller's details, what they asked and what was booked or passed on.

How do I change prices, hours or policies later?

Tell us what changed by email or text and we update the receptionist's information. Your team can ask for changes at any time.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on your call volume and which tools we connect. We'll give you a clear quote after the strategy call, with no obligation.
