SERVICES

The whole front desk, handled by AI.

Three things your receptionist does on every call, and the four steps we take to get it live on your phone line.

Answers every call

Picks up on the first ring, day or night, greets callers by your business name, and holds a natural conversation. No hold music, no phone trees.

  • Answers after hours, weekends and holidays
  • Handles several calls at once
  • Takes chat and text messages too, if you want them

Follows your rules

Answers only from information you approve: services, prices, hours, policies. Emergencies, complaints and anything you flag go straight to a person.

  • Never guesses prices or promises
  • Hands off to your team when you say so
  • Says what it can't answer and when a person will follow up

Books and reports

Checks your live calendar, books the slot, sends the caller a confirmation, and texts or emails your team a summary of every call.

  • Live calendar availability
  • Confirmation by email or text
  • A summary of every call for your team
HOW WE SET IT UP

Live on your phone line
in four simple steps.

Free strategy call

We learn how calls reach your business today, what callers ask most, and when calls are being missed.

Script & rules

We write your receptionist's greeting, answers and rules: services, prices, hours, what it must never say, and when to hand off to a person.

Connect & test

We connect your calendar, spreadsheet or CRM and set up call forwarding from your existing number. You make test calls before any customer does.

Go live & improve

After launch we review real calls with you and adjust answers and rules until it handles calls the way you want.
